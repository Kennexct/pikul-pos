All colour tokens, radius, shadow and motion

Radius sm 6, md 8, lg 10, xl 14. Shadows xs (cards), pop (sheets, toasts, cart bar), bar. Eases out-quart (enter), in-quart (exit). Utilities num, animate-sheet-in/out, animate-dialog-*, scroll-x.

Rules

Full text in design-system/MASTER.md.

  • Speed over decoration: 44px targets, 48px for primary actions.
  • Borders give structure; only sheets, toasts and the cart bar float.
  • One accent, pandan green. Status colours only for a real status.
  • The product photo is the only colour on a tile.
  • A dashed receipt rule sits above every money total, nowhere else.
  • Every amount uses num so Rupiah columns align.
  • Weights up to 600, sentence case labels, no pills, emoji or loops.
